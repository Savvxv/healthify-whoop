HEALTHIFY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Healthify is an independent fitness and wellbeing app in development, operated by Mustafa Tariq. This policy describes its planned WHOOP integration. Publishing this page does not itself connect your WHOOP account or collect your WHOOP health records.

Information you choose to connect

If you authorize Healthify through WHOOP, Healthify may receive the information covered by the permissions you approve: your WHOOP account identifier and basic profile, sleep, recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, physiological cycles, workouts, strain, and body measurements, where available and authorized. OAuth access and refresh tokens enable authorized access. Healthify does not ask for your WHOOP password; you sign in with WHOOP.

How information is used

Authorized information is used to display your fitness metrics, show trends and personal progress, and support Healthify's fitness progression features. Healthify is not a medical service. Data access must remain limited to the permissions you grant.

Storage and access

The app's hosting and storage arrangements are still being developed. Before inviting users to connect, this policy will be updated to identify the active storage providers, retention periods, and applicable account controls. Do not submit health information through this public website or the public GitHub repository. Health records, OAuth tokens, and client secrets must never be committed to this repository.

Sharing

Healthify does not sell WHOOP data or use it for targeted advertising. No friend, leaderboard, or public profile sharing is enabled by this policy. Any future sharing features will require a separate choice and an updated explanation. WHOOP handles its own service under its privacy policy. This public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement.

Your choices and deletion

Connecting WHOOP is optional. You can revoke Healthify's authorization through WHOOP's connected integration controls. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not by itself delete information previously stored by an app. To request information about access or deletion of Healthify-held data, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile below. Use only a non-sensitive initial inquiry and request a private contact channel; never post health records, tokens, passwords, or account identifiers in public issues.

Development status and changes

Healthify is currently in development. A working connection, account management, and deletion controls must be implemented before use by other people. This policy will be revised as the service is implemented; the date above identifies the current version.
